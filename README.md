The market for used and new vehicles is very versitle and can be extremely overwhelming if the customer as no prior experience. This is why we will train our algorithim using the cars dataset to predict the price of a vehicle based on it's features, such as engine, color, drivetrain, interior, exterior, make & model. Using our prediction we will either be above or lower then the actual listed priced of the car. There will be 2 cases granted our predictions are accurate. Either higher or lower. If we predict the value of the car to be higher then the listing of the car we will consider this good. This means our prediction has gotten the consumer a good deal on a vehicle. If we predict the vehicle to be lower value then the listed price this is also good becausse we have saved the customer from an overpriced car. If our predictions of the vehicle are inccorect then there is the possibility of a customer missing out on a good deal or buying an overpriced car. There is close to 4 million cars sold a year and with inflation of roughly 40% on the car market our prediction can help save alot of money. If even geting a good deal on the car for $100 would already be $400 million in savings.
